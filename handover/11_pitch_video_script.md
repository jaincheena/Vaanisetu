# VaaniSetu — 1-Minute Pitch Video Script
## Hackathon Panel Presentation

> **Target Length:** 60 seconds (approx. 130 words)
> **Pacing:** Upbeat, professional, and impactful. Pause slightly at the bolded words.
> **Format:** This storyboard provides the visual cues and the exact voiceover script. You can record this using Canva, PowerPoint, or basic video editing software with screen recordings of the app.

### Tips for Production:
1. **Screen Recordings:** Record the real VaaniSetu app working. Show the green/dark glassmorphism UI—it looks premium and will impress the judges.
2. **Music:** Use a subtle, driving instrumental background track (like a light corporate tech cinematic track). Duck the volume (lower it) while the voiceover speaks.
3. **Pacing:** Don't rush the voiceover. If it takes 65 seconds, that is perfectly fine. Clarity is more important than speed.
