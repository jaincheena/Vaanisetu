🌱 Solution Overview

VaaniSetu (meaning "voice bridge") is a fully offline AI-powered translation platform built for BAIF Development Research Foundation. It translates agricultural training videos, audio, and text into 22 official Indian languages without requiring internet after setup.

Why Was It Built?

BAIF's field content reaches farming communities across India, but professional translation is expensive (₹850+/min), slow, and unavailable offline. VaaniSetu brings AI translation into the office — instantly, cost-free at runtime, and fully private.

🏗️ Architecture & Flow (Simplified)

Rather than complex technical diagrams, it's easier to think of VaaniSetu as a seamless, high-speed assembly line running locally on your office computer.

The Journey of a Video

  1. The Intake (File Upload)
    You upload a training video. The system immediately strips away the video picture to focus completely on the audio track.
  2. The Listener (Transcription)
    Our AI "Listener" (called Whisper) carefully listens to the audio and writes everything down into sentences, noting exactly what second each sentence was spoken.
  3. Checking the Vault (Translation Memory)
    This is the smartest part of the system. Before translating, VaaniSetu checks its internal vault. If someone at BAIF has translated this exact same sentence before, it pulls the human-approved version out of the vault to save time and guarantee quality!
  4. The Translator (AI Translation)
    For any new sentences not in the vault, the AI steps in. It translates the sentence and assigns a Confidence Score.
    • If it is very sure, it marks it Green.
    • If it is slightly unsure, it marks it Amber for you to double-check.
  5. The Assembly Line (Outputs)
    The system then packages the new text into PDFs, Word Documents, Subtitle files, and even generates a synthetic voice track. It zips them all together in a single folder ready for you to download!

⚙️ Setup & Installation

Method A — Internet Setup

Step 1: Check Prerequisites

Run scripts\check_hardware.bat — all items must show [PASS]

  • Windows 11 64-bit
  • 16 GB RAM, 200 GB free disk
  • Python 3.11+, Node.js 18+, FFmpeg in PATH

Step 2: Install Software

Run scripts\setup.bat — installs Python packages + builds React UI (~30 min)

Step 3: Download Models

Run scripts\download_models.bat — downloads ~6-8 GB of AI models

Step 4: Start Server

Run scripts\start_vaanisetu.bat — browser opens automatically when ready

Method B — USB Offline Install

Prepare USB on an internet PC first (run Method A, then copy files to USB). On offline PC, run install_from_usb.bat from the USB drive.

❓ Staff FAQ

Plain-English answers to common questions from training officers, field coordinators, content reviewers, and IT admins.

A — Getting Started

How do I open VaaniSetu? Do I need to install anything?

No installation needed. Open any browser and type the office server address (e.g. http://192.168.1.100:8765). Your IT admin will give you the exact address — save it as a bookmark. Works on laptops, tablets, and phones connected to the office WiFi.

Do I need an account or password?

No. VaaniSetu has no login. Anyone on the office WiFi can open it.

What file types can I upload?

TypeFormats
Video.mp4, .mkv, .avi, .mov, .webm
Audio.mp3, .wav, .m4a, .ogg, .flac
Text.txt
Maximum size2 GB per file
Cannot upload PowerPoint or Image files. For unsupported document types, use the new "Short Text" mode to paste text.

The server address doesn’t open. What do I do?

  1. Check you’re on office WiFi (not mobile data)
  2. Check the server computer is switched on
  3. Check IT admin has started the server (start_vaanisetu.bat)
  4. Try refreshing (F5)
  5. If still failing — contact IT admin

Translate Content vs Reverse Bridge — which do I use?

ModeUse when…
Translate ContentYou have BAIF training material and want to translate it into regional languages for farmers
Reverse BridgeA farmer recorded a query in their local language and you want to understand it in English for HQ experts

Tip: Submit long videos before lunch or at end of day. Download when you return.

Can I close the browser while a job is running?

Yes. The job continues on the server. Come back to History later and download the ZIP when it shows “Completed.”

Can two people submit jobs at the same time?

Yes — both are accepted and queued. Jobs process one at a time. Queue position is shown in the top banner.

D — Review Queue

Who should do the reviews?

Bilingual staff or volunteers who speak both the source and the target language. No technical knowledge needed — they just read and judge whether the translation sounds correct.

Do I have to review every sentence?

No. Only Amber and Red sentences appear. Green sentences (typically 70–80%) are auto-accepted. You review only the uncertain 20–30%.

How do I use the Review Queue?

  1. Click Review Queue in the sidebar
  2. Enter your name in the “Reviewer Name” box
  3. For each card: read source (left), read translation (right)
  4. Choose: ✓ Approve / ✏️ Save Edit / ✗ Reject

Your corrections are saved permanently — the AI will use them in all future jobs.

E — Privacy & Safety

Does farmer audio get sent to Google or any outside server?

No. Everything stays on the BAIF office computer. No internet is used during translation. No farmer data ever leaves your building.

Is it safe to translate dosage or chemical safety content?

⚠️ For any content involving pesticide dosages, chemical usage, or medical advice — always require a qualified bilingual reviewer to check the Review Queue before distributing, even if the badge shows Green.

F — Troubleshooting

Top banner says “Models: Loading…”

Normal at startup — AI models take 2–5 minutes to load. Wait and refresh. If still loading after 10 minutes, contact IT admin.

Error: “Unsupported file type”

PDF, Word, PowerPoint, and image files are not accepted. Copy your text into Notepad, save as .txt, and upload that instead.

Job shows “failed” in History

Possible causes: corrupted file, silent video, or server ran out of memory. Try re-uploading. If it fails again, contact IT admin with the job ID shown in History.

Top banner shows “Disk: low” in red

Tell IT admin immediately. New jobs may fail until old files are cleared. IT admin can fix this in under 10 minutes.

Regional language text shows as boxes/question marks in Word

Font issue on the viewer’s computer. Select the text in Word and change the font to Mangal (Hindi/Devanagari) or Nirmala UI (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali). Windows 11 includes these fonts by default.

G — Impact Ledger & Glossary

What is the Impact Ledger?

Automatically tracks hours translated, money saved vs. professional agencies, and farmers reachable. Click 📄 Export PDF to get a report for donor submissions.

The “farmers reachable” number looks wrong. Can it be adjusted?

Yes. The Rate Configuration table at the bottom of the Impact Ledger page lets your programme manager adjust the multipliers per language to match your field data.

What is the Glossary?

A dictionary of terms BAIF has translated 3+ times with high confidence — automatically built from your jobs. Click 📄 Export DOCX to share it with external language reviewers.
